Run order for day one

Hour by hour, left to right. Elapsed times are honest estimates, not commitments — every one is INFERRED.

Pull every schema/definition/setup table reachable before touching a single transaction: chart of accounts, account-schedule definitions, department suffix conventions, pay-type/labour-op codes, parts source/price codes, document types, and the user/permission inventory itself. Metadata before transactions, always.

Every transaction class for the 3-month window (May, June, July 2026 by default — confirm exact months on the day): repair orders (header/labour/parts/punches), counter parts sales, deal jackets, parts orders & receipts, warranty claims, and the GL journal/schedule exports that make the ledger visible at all.

Tie-out happens on the middle month of the window (June 2026 by default). This board does not rebuild the financials — that lives on Lane C's tie-out board, linked below. What this phase needs from the extract is the control totals captured in the counts & integrity panel further down this page.
