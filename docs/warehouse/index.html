The join spine is VIN8

The single most consequential finding in this extract set. CDK's Vehicle ID on the repair order carries the 8-character VIN suffix, and every PACCAR warranty-registration row is keyed on VIN8. The manufacturer record and the dealer record meet on a real key, not a fuzzy name match.

Out of scope
Powersports lane
BRP contracts, Warranty on Demand claims and Torque retention reports — a separate VIN space, logged as SCOPE_EXCLUDED and never loaded
Registered-owner name is not a join key — only 65 of 534 manufacturer-side owner names match a CDK repair-order customer name exactly. The secondary spine is the customer number, recovered from CDK's legacy Pick NAME-FILE key, which resolves 818 of the 821 repair-order customers back to the customer master.

Data quality — nothing is silently dropped

Every rejection lands in dq_exception with a rule code and a severity. The counts are reproducible by rerunning the loader.

RuleSeverityRowsMeaning
VIN8_LENGTHWARN1,223RO rows whose vehicle ID is neither 8 nor 17 characters, so they cannot reach a warranty registration
DATE_PARSEWARN213registration rows with no usable in-service date, so coverage end cannot be computed
RO_DUPLICATEWARN12RO number present in both the open and closed extract; the closed row wins
ROOFTOP_CLOSED_HISTORY_MISSINGWARN5a service account present in the open-RO extract with no closed history extracted, so its sales and mileage history are absent
SCOPE_EXCLUDEDINFO6a file in the extract directory belonging to the BRP powersports lane, deliberately not loaded
CUST_UNKNOWNINFO3customer on an RO absent from the customer master; a stub dimension row is created
245 of the 2,436 distinct CDK vehicle IDs are six characters long. They cannot be lengthened by inference and must never be fuzzy-matched to a VIN8 — a wrong match would attach one fleet's coverage to another fleet's truck. They are quarantined and counted, which is why the mart population is smaller than the repair-order population.

Gaps in the extract set

Named plainly, because the next extract request should be shaped by them.

  • No repair-order line detail. These are RO headers only. Labour operation codes, SRT hours, part numbers and the 3-C complaint text are absent, so per-job margin estimate and 3-C compliance cannot yet be scored. This is the single highest-value addition to the next request.
  • No claim-side data on the Peterbilt lane. PRWS claim history is not in this set, so booked-versus-eligible cannot be closed on the truck side yet. The 205 Warranty on Demand claim records in the extract directory are BRP powersports claims and do not fill this gap.
  • Closed repair-order history covers three of eight service accounts. The five without history hold 169 of 260 open repair orders and $656,069.67 of $832,310.28 in open work in progress, so the $12.11M closed-sales figure is a floor for the group rather than the group. Closed ROs for all eight accounts over the same window is ask one of the next extract request.
  • No SmartLINQ or PACCAR Solutions fault-code feed, so the event-driven telemetry rung is absent from the model.
  • Odometer arrives only via repair orders. Units never serviced here have no mileage reading, so their mileage axis is reported as null — never as zero.
